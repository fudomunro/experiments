## Personal Care & Hygiene

We provide respectful assistance with personal care tasks to help our clients feel their best every day.

- Bathing, showering, grooming, hair care, and shaving
- Dressing assistance and support with prosthetics or braces
- Colostomy care and other specialized hygiene needs
- Mobility support (walkers, wheelchairs, and cane use)

## Light Housekeeping

Maintaining a clean and organized home is essential for safety and peace of mind. Our caregivers assist with:

- Laundry: personal clothes and bedding
- Dishes, cleaning floors, and dusting
- Cleaning the fridge and vacuuming
- General home safety checks and security

![Senior in a clean home environment](images/senior-tea.jpg)

</section>

## Medication & Communication

We ensure that medications are taken correctly and that family members remain fully informed about their loved one's well-being.

- **Medication Reminders**: Timely reminders and dispersing meds from bubble packs.
- **Charting**: We record and log medications and provide a relevant, up-to-date care plan.
- **Family Communication**: Open communication books and regular updates to identified family contacts.
- **Refill Coordination**: Reminding family contacts when medication refills are needed.

</section>
