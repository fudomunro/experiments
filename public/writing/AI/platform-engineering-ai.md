## Platform Engineering

Platform Engineering is all about trade-offs and living with incomplete implementations. Successful platform engineering leaders support thriving developers despite platform flaws, not because they have been eliminated.

But AI assisted development substantially reduces the cost of certain types of engineering, many of which are relevant to building a development platform.

Perhaps things are different now?
