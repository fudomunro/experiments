# Learning Portuguese 

With help from Thiago

## Getting Started

I went to Duolingo first, as it's what I'm most familiar with.

First day was pretty good. Lots of ads, but made steady progress.

Second day I had several issues with running out of energy or the app crashing near the end of exercises. It was frustrating, so I decided to try something else.

I found Busuu, which was well reviewed. Initially it also had a lot of ads, so I tried out the 7 day premium trial.

Been making steady progress with Busuu, except for one thing. They have a type of exercise that gets graded by other users, so I have to type or speak the answer and wait for feedback. I find this generally very intimidating for anything where I don't know the answer, so several times I've stopped progress when I hit one of these.

## Back to Duolingo

After a couple of days of doing nothing because I was intimidated of Busuu, I went back to Duolingo and paid for a month.

It's been mostly good, but I do find the application itself to be really annoying. Like, even when I'm paying and I adjust the settings, the number of prompts I have to interact with between activities is excessive:
 - Share results to social media
 - You're 73% towards the Busy Beaver badge
 - Skip video calling activities, which are the most common type, but you have to pay extra for

But in terms of the process of learning Portuguese, I feel like I'm picking up a good amount of vocabulary at a steady pace, with at least some practice on grammar and pronounciation.

## Doing something with it

I need another way to practice it though, so I'm going to translate my writings here into Portuguese.

I pick an article, and then go through line-by-line and write my guess of what the translation should look like, then paste the line into Google translate and compare to my guess.
